# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Industrial, mining, food-industry and general-fabrication buyers assessing whether BVA can understand and deliver a project. They need to establish capability, relevance and a practical contact path quickly.

## Product Purpose

This L0 local concept preview demonstrates a more credible, BVA-specific way to present public manufacturing capability. Success is a buyer quickly understanding BVA's scope and choosing a direct phone enquiry.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, direct. It should feel like a well-used capability statement from a serious South West workshop—not a startup, luxury editorial site or generic AI industrial landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid AI-green-on-black generic workshop imagery as the whole identity; generic startup claims; repeated card grids; decorative all-caps labels; rounded-SaaS surfaces; fake RFQ workflows; unsupported quality, project or certification claims.

## Design Principles

1. Put the real BVA identity and machine capability ahead of generic atmosphere.
2. Let facts, dimensions and industrial sectors do the persuasive work.
3. Create a strong capability-statement rhythm, not a conventional landing-page template.
4. Preserve a single practical action: a direct call to the workshop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation, descriptive image text, responsive reading order and reduced-motion support. No form or data collection exists in this L0 concept.
