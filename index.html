One workshop.
Many ways to make metal work.

BVA is a family-run Picton fabrication workshop servicing the South West and projects further afield. The team works across general fabrication, structural steel, stainless, aluminium, laser cutting and site services.

Workshop capability,
made clear.

From a small repair to workshop fabrication, BVA’s published service range brings cutting, forming, welding and site support into one local conversation.

Structural & general steel

Built for access, plant and industrial work.

  • Platforms, ladders, walkways and conveyors
  • Pipework, augers, tank construction and repairs
  • Industrial ducting, shed flashings and sheet-metal repairs
  • Specialised toolboxes and general fabrication

Stainless, aluminium & food industry

Specialised fabrication where finish and fit matter.

  • Stainless benches, kitchen exhaust canopies and shelving
  • Trolleys, sinks, industrial tanks and ducting
  • Stainless steel and aluminium fabrication
  • MIG, TIG and ARC welding for stainless and aluminium

Laser cutting & forming

Cut, etch, guillotine, roll and bend.

  • Laser cutting and laser etching on a 4000 × 2000 mm table
  • Carbon steel up to 16 mm; stainless and aluminium up to 10 mm
  • Guillotining, rolling and bending for plate and sheet
  • Profile rolling for angle, flanges, SHS, pipe and flat bar

Site services & repairs

Workshop capability when the work is on site.

  • Plant maintenance, fabrication and installation
  • Construction and shutdown crews
  • Asset integrity and breakdown services
  • Minor repairs through to major repair work out on site
